# Weather Fortress Watch Face Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Weather Fortress Watch Face does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer. The watch face contains no advertising, analytics, account system, or developer-operated server.

The watch face can display time, date, watch battery level, step count, heart rate, temperature, weather condition, and chance of precipitation. These values are supplied by the Wear OS device, its sensors, system services, or a supported weather provider. They are displayed on the watch and are not received or stored by the developer.

Availability of health and weather information depends on the user's device, permissions, settings, network connection, and service provider. The watch face is intended for general information and personalization only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Weather Fortress Watch Face is suitable for a general audience, including children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

For privacy questions, contact [summer.work.001@gmail.com](mailto:summer.work.001@gmail.com).

# 天气城堡表盘隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 8 月 10 日

天气城堡表盘不会向开发者收集、存储、传输、出售或共享任何个人数据。本表盘不包含广告、分析工具、账号系统或由开发者运营的服务器。

表盘可以显示时间、日期、手表电量、步数、心率、温度、天气状态和降水概率。这些数据由 Wear OS 设备、设备传感器、系统服务或受支持的天气服务提供，仅显示在手表上，不会由开发者接收或存储。

健康与天气信息是否可用，取决于用户的设备、权限、设置、网络连接及服务提供方。本表盘仅用于日常信息展示和个性化，不提供医疗建议、诊断或治疗。

天气城堡表盘适合包括儿童在内的一般用户。开发者不会有意收集儿童或成人的个人数据。
